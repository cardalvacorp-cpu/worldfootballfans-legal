Privacy Policy for World Football Fans

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

Introduction

World Football Fans ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: Team preferences (country and club), profile description, profile picture, team shield
  • Content: Posts, photos, videos, comments, reactions (green/red/yellow cards)
  • Location Data: Geographic location (only if you enable location sharing and confirm you are 18+)
  • Age Verification: Confirmation that you are 18 years or older

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, posts viewed, interactions with content
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, app crashes and errors

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the World Football Fans app
  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your posts, profile, and location on the map (if enabled)
  • Connect you with other football fans based on team preferences
  • Send notifications about app activity
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to reports of inappropriate content
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and community guidelines
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Other Users: Your profile, posts, location (if enabled), and public interactions are visible to other app users
  • Service Providers: Cloud storage, analytics, and hosting services that help us operate the app
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

Your Data Rights

  • Access: View your personal data
  • Edit: Update your profile and settings
  • Delete: Request account deletion (Settings > Delete Account)
  • Control Location: Enable/disable location sharing anytime
  • Privacy Settings: Hide your profile from other users

Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage
  • Secure data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Regular security updates
  • Reported content moderation

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Age Restrictions

You must be 18 years or older to use location sharing features. We require age confirmation during signup. If we learn that a user under 18 has shared their location, we will disable that feature immediately.

Location Data

  • Location sharing is optional and requires age verification (18+)
  • You can enable/disable location sharing in Settings
  • Your location is displayed on the map to other users when enabled
  • We do not track your location history

Content Moderation

  • Users can report inappropriate posts
  • We review reported content and may remove posts or ban users who violate our guidelines
  • Reported posts and user reports are stored for moderation purposes

Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained while your account exists
  • Deleted posts are removed from public view but may be retained for moderation (30 days)
  • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days

Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage, analytics
  • OpenStreetMap: Map services
  • ExoPlayer (Google): Video playback
  • Glide: Image loading

These services have their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or email.

Your Consent
